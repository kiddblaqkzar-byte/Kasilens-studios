Who We Are

KasiLens Studios is a South African photography and videography company founded by Tatise Buffel. Established in 2026, the company was created with a passion for capturing life's most meaningful moments through high-quality photography, cinematic videography, and creative storytelling.

From portraits and family sessions to weddings, community events, corporate functions, and music videos, KasiLens Studios is committed to delivering professional, affordable, and memorable visual experiences. Every project is approached with creativity, dedication, and attention to detail, ensuring that each client's story is captured beautifully.

Guided by our slogan, "Capturing the Heart of Mzansi," we proudly celebrate South Africa's people, culture, and unforgettable moments—one frame at a time.

Our Mission

To provide affordable, high-quality photography and videography services while capturing the heart of Mzansi through every image and video we create.
